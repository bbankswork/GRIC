Help Keep Your HomeSecure While You're Away

Planning to be away from home? Taking a few precautions before you leave can help reduce opportunities for theft and make it easier for someone to notice a problem at your property.

The Gila River Police Department offers Vacation House Checks to help residents look after their homes while they are away. This service is part of GRPD’s commitment to crime prevention and the safety of the Gila River Indian Community. Learn how to request a check and what information to provide before leaving home.

Home Safety Tips

Before You Leave

🔒

Secure Doors and Windows

Double-check that exterior doors, windows, gates, and outbuildings are locked.

💡

Make the Home Look Occupied

Consider timers for lights and avoid leaving obvious signs that the home is vacant.

🤝

Ask a Trusted Person to Help

Arrange for a trusted neighbor, relative, or friend to collect mail, watch for unusual activity, and contact you if something seems wrong.

💎

Keep Valuables Out of Sight

Store valuable items securely and avoid leaving them visible from windows.

📹

Review Alarms and Cameras

If you have a security system, check that it is working and that trusted contacts know how to reach you.

📱

Share a Contact Plan

Make sure a trusted person has your contact information and knows when you expect to return.

How Do I Request a Vacation House Check?

To request a Vacation House Check, call GRPD Non-Emergency Dispatch at (520) 562-4511 before you leave. Be prepared to provide your home address, the dates you will be away, and contact information in case officers need to reach you.

What If I Notice Suspicious Activity?

Report suspicious activity or other non-emergency police concerns to GRPD at 520-562-4511. If there is an immediate emergency or a crime in progress, call 911.
