RAVE Alert System

Emergency CommunityNotification

The RAVE Alert System is the Gila River Indian Community (GRIC) Public Safety Division's official emergency alert and notification system. It helps notify the public during emergencies, including severe weather, public safety concerns, traffic alerts, missing persons, and evacuations.

RAVE alerts are a free service. You can register online to receive customized alerts by text message, email, and voice message.

You may also choose to receive Community notifications about safety, health, utility disruptions, and other relevant topics. The service is provided by GRIC at no cost, although message and data rates may apply.

Frequently Asked Questions

Alerts powered by Smart911 are available to anyone who lives, works, travels through, or visits the Gila River Indian Community. You can register an address for your home, work, or another location you care about.

Alert frequency depends on the addresses you provide, your selected notification types, and actual emergency conditions. Emergency alerts are issued when there is an immediate threat to life or property. Community notifications are sent when their alert criteria are met.

You can adjust your selections at any time through your Smart911 account.

Information submitted through the alert system is private, exempt from public disclosure, and used only for emergency purposes.

  • Visit the Smart911 sign-in page.
  • Sign in with your username and password. Use the account recovery options if needed.
  • Open the Preferences tab.
  • Update the phone numbers and email addresses where you receive alerts.
  • Choose which types of alerts to receive and whether you prefer text, voice, or email notifications.

For example, you can turn off transportation disruption notifications entirely or choose to receive those notifications by email only.
