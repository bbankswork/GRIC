Blue Envelope Program

Supporting Clearer CommunicationDuring Traffic Stops

Arizona's Blue Envelope Program is a free, voluntary initiative that helps autistic drivers communicate with law enforcement officers during traffic stops. The blue envelope provides a recognizable way to keep important vehicle documents together and includes guidance to support a calmer, clearer interaction.

The program was developed through a partnership involving the Arizona Department of Transportation Motor Vehicle Division, the Arizona Department of Public Safety, and the University of Arizona.

How It Works

How Does the Blue Envelope Program Work?

1

Get a Blue Envelope

Request a free envelope from an Arizona MVD office, authorized third-party office, or listed distribution location.

2

Keep Important Documents Together

Place your current vehicle registration and insurance information inside. You may also include emergency contact information.

3

Store It in Your Vehicle

Keep the envelope in your glove compartment or another location that is easy to access when requested during a traffic stop.

4

Share It During a Traffic Stop

Present the envelope to the officer. The printed guidance helps explain that you may communicate or respond differently during stressful situations.

Where Can You Get a Blue Envelope?

Blue Envelopes are available free of charge at Arizona MVD and authorized third-party offices, as well as participating law enforcement and community locations. You do not need to provide medical records or register to receive an envelope.
