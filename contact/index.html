Call Tips: Make the Right Call

The Gila River Police Department can respond more effectively when you Make the Right Call. Not all situations require 911. For non-emergencies, use the designated non-emergency number (520) 562-4511 to ensure resources are allocated appropriately.

Emergency Services: When Should You Call 911?

Call 911 to report emergencies involving:

  • A crime in progress
  • An imminent threat to life or safety
  • Bodily injury
  • Major property damage or loss
Call 911 →

Non-Emergency Situations: When Should You Call the Non-Emergency Number?

For non-emergency police assistance, call (520) 562-4511. Non-emergency situations include incidents that:

  • Do not involve a crime in progress
  • Do not pose an imminent threat to life or safety
  • Do not involve major property damage
Call (520) 562-4511 →

How to Describe a Suspect

Providing an accurate and detailed description of a suspect is crucial for law enforcement. Key details to share include:

  • Gender, race, and age
  • Height, weight, and build
  • Clothing description (type, color, patterns)
  • Distinctive features (tattoos, scars, facial hair, etc.)
  • Vehicle description (make, model, color, license plate if possible)
  • Direction of travel
