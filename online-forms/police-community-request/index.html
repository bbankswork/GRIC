Request GRPD Attendance at Your Community Event

The Gila River Police Department is proud to support and engage with our Community beyond law enforcement duties. If you or your organization would like to request GRPD’s participation at an event in a community relations capacity (not for security or law enforcement presence), please complete the request form below.

While we strive to honor as many requests as possible, attendance is not guaranteed and depends on officer availability, staffing levels, and calls for service. Weekend events may be especially limited due to scheduling constraints.

!Please wait for formal confirmation before advertising GRPD’s attendance at your event.

Thank you for partnering with us to strengthen Community connections.
