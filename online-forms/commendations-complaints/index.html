File a Complaint

Your Concerns Matter

The Gila River Police Department is committed to professionalism, accountability, and treating people with dignity and respect. If you believe a GRPD employee did not meet these standards, you can submit a complaint.

The Professional Standards Unit is responsible for reviewing allegations of employee misconduct and investigating what occurred. Complaints may be handled by a supervisor or the Professional Standards Unit depending on the circumstances.

!Please provide as much accurate information as possible, including the date, time, location, individuals involved, and any witnesses. Intentionally false complaints may be subject to applicable law.
