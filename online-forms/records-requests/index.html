Police Records

Requesting Police Records

The Gila River Police Department Records Division maintains reports of crimes and incidents occurring within the Gila River Indian Community. The division assists with records requests and the release of police reports when appropriate.

!The Gila River Indian Community is a federally recognized Indian tribe. Arizona open records laws generally do not apply within the Community, and Tribal records are not subject to the federal Freedom of Information Act or Federal Privacy Act.

How to Request a Police Report

Personal Requests

You can request a police report in person at GRPD Headquarters or contact the Records Division at (520) 562-7140 or (520) 562-7540.

Victims of a crime may receive one free copy of a report in which they are listed as the victim. Otherwise, a police report copy costs $5.00.

Business Requests

Submit a request on business letterhead by mail to:

Gila River Police Department
P.O. Box 2186
Sacaton, AZ 85147

If requesting multiple items, contact the Records Division to confirm availability and the correct fees before sending payment. Incorrect payment can delay the request. Separate payments for each item are optional and may help prevent delays.
