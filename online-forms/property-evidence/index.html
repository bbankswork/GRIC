About theProperty & Evidence Unit

The Property & Evidence Unit is responsible for maintaining the integrity of evidence and the chain of custody for property that comes into the possession of the Gila River Police Department, in accordance with state laws, tribal ordinances, and department policies and procedures. Evidence personnel receive, store, release, and maintain the security of a wide variety of items.

📦 Claiming Property or Evidence

Release of Property & Evidence

Evidence includes items connected to a crime, such as tools used during a burglary or property recovered from a burglary. Evidence is handled and stored differently from other property because it may be required during criminal proceedings.

Items held as evidence are generally retained until prosecution has concluded for all parties involved in the case.

!IMPORTANT: Approval is required from the Tribal Courts, state courts, and/or the Police Officer or Detective assigned to the case before property can be released.

What to Bring When Claiming Your Property

  • A valid, unexpired government-issued ID
  • Your case number
  • Proof of ownership for firearms and high-value items, when requested

🧾 Proof of Ownership

How Can I Show Proof of Ownership?

For found property, bicycles, and recovered stolen property, proof of ownership may include:

  • The item's serial number
  • A dated purchase receipt
  • A prior stolen or lost property report filed with a police agency
  • A photograph showing you with the item
  • A description of unique identifying characteristics
  • An access code or combination that unlocks the item

🔒 Release of Firearms

Release of Firearms

!IMPORTANT: Firearm returns are by appointment only. The Property & Evidence Unit does not release firearms to walk-in visitors.

Under the department's stated Tribal ordinance requirements, anyone reclaiming a firearm from a law enforcement agency must undergo a GRPD background check.

To claim a firearm, bring:

  • Your government-issued ID
  • A locking gun case or gun lock
  • Proof of ownership, such as a sales receipt or a notarized statement for a private-party purchase

GRPD requires all firearms, including pistols and long guns, to be registered before release. A firearm held as evidence may also require clearance from the investigating officer, Gila River Tribal Court, federal prosecutors, or a state court agency.

Ammunition seized with a firearm is disposed of according to department policy. GRPD does not return seized ammunition.

🗄️ Safekeeping & Unclaimed Property

Safekeeping and Unclaimed Property

Safekeeping items are held for an owner for reasons other than their use as evidence in a criminal case.

Weapons taken for safekeeping in connection with a domestic violence incident or psychiatric evaluation may be retrieved only after applicable conditions are met and the necessary clearance has been obtained from the Gila River Tribal Court and/or investigating officer.

Items held for safekeeping, found property, recovered property, and cleared stolen property may be returned to their owners. Proof of ownership may be required.

Unclaimed property is disposed of according to Community ordinances and applicable state statutes.

🔎 Found Property

Found Property

If you find an item valued at more than $100, contact the Gila River Police Department as soon as possible. It may have been stolen or involved in an incident under investigation.

Found property can be turned over to a police officer or brought to a GRPD police facility.

⚠️ Discarding Weapons & Ammunition

Discarding Weapons and Ammunition

!Firearms must be unloaded before transport. Do not bring a loaded firearm into a police facility.

The department's existing instructions allow residents to bring unwanted rifles, shotguns, handguns, ammunition, and other weapons to a police facility for destruction.

If you have a loaded firearm and are unsure how to unload it safely, do not attempt to unload it yourself. Call GRPD non-emergency dispatch at (520) 562-4511, explain the situation, and request officer assistance.

If you discover a firearm or another weapon outdoors, do not touch or disturb it. Call GRPD non-emergency dispatch at (520) 562-4511 so an officer can respond. The item may be evidence in a police investigation.
