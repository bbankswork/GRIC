Missing Persons

Help Bring Them Home

The Gila River Police Department shares missing person bulletins to help locate Community members and bring them home safely. If you have seen someone in the bulletins below or have any information, please contact GRPD right away.

There is no waiting period to report a missing person. If you believe someone is missing, contact GRPD immediately.

!If the missing person is a child, an elderly or vulnerable adult, or may be in danger, call 911 immediately.
