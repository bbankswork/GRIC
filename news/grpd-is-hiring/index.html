GRPD Is Hiring: $7,500 Bonus for New Recruits and Lateral Officers

October 30, 2026 · Gila River Police Department

The Gila River Police Department is hiring Police Officer Recruits, Lateral Transfers, and Civilian Professional Staff to serve the Gila River Indian Community.

Eligible new Police Officer Recruits and Lateral Transfers receive a $7,500 hiring bonus, paid upon successful completion of the academy and field training.

No Experience Required

Police Officer Recruit positions are designed for applicants starting their law enforcement careers. GRPD provides academy training and field training. Recruits attend the 16-week Southern Arizona Law Enforcement Training Center academy in Tucson, which provides lodging and meals.

“Stand Strong. Serve Proud.”

Who Can Apply

  • Applicants must be at least 21 years old by the time they graduate from the police academy. There is no upper age cutoff to apply.
  • Certified officers can apply for Police Officer Lateral Transfer positions.
  • Everyone is welcome to apply. Indian preference is part of the Community's hiring process.
