First Responder Cadet Program

Interested in Law Enforcement?Start Exploring Your Future.

Not old enough to become a police officer yet? You can still begin exploring a future in public safety.

The Gila River Indian Community's First Responder Cadet Program introduces young people to the work of public safety professionals, including the Gila River Police Department. Through learning opportunities and hands-on activities, participants can gain insight into what it takes to serve their Community and consider careers in law enforcement and other emergency response fields.

The program has been offered through a collaboration involving GRPD, the Gila River Fire Department, emergency medical services, and the Community's Employment & Training youth program.

Career Exploration

A First Step Toward a Careerin Public Safety

The Cadet Program can help participants learn about the day-to-day work of first responders before deciding what direction they want to pursue. Earlier Gila River programs have brought cadets together with police officers, firefighters, and emergency services personnel for practical learning and mentorship.

Even if you are still deciding on a career, exploring these opportunities can help you better understand the skills, training, and commitment involved in public safety.

Who Can Participate?

The First Responder Cadet Program serves young members of the Gila River Indian Community. Contact the Employment & Training Youth Program for current age requirements, session dates, and participation details.

How Do I Join the Cadet Program?

The Gila River Indian Community Employment & Training Youth Program is an established point of contact for First Responder Program information. Reach out to ask about the next session, eligibility, and application steps.

When Can I Apply to Become a Police Officer?

GRPD's Police Officer Recruit requirements state that applicants must be at least 21 years old by the time they graduate from the police academy. If you do not meet that requirement yet, the Cadet Program can be an opportunity to learn more about public safety and prepare for future career decisions.

!Participation in the Cadet Program does not guarantee employment or admission to the police academy.
