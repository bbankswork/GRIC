Sex Offender Registration and Public Notification

The Gila River Police Department provides sex offender information pursuant to the Gila River Indian Community Sex Offender Registration and Notification Law and the Adam Walsh Child Protection and Safety Act of 2006. Public notification is intended to support safety and awareness within the Gila River Indian Community.

This information is intended to inform Community members, not increase public fear.

!Appearance on this page DOES NOT INDICATE that a person is wanted by the police. Sex Offender Registry information shall not be used to commit a crime or to engage in illegal discrimination or harassment of an offender.

Who Must Register?

According to GRPD's current published instructions, offenders must register in any jurisdiction where they live, work (whether paid or unpaid), attend school or vocational/college education, or temporarily reside in the Community for more than three days.

Failure to register may have federal and Community legal consequences. Residence restrictions and possible exclusion procedures may also apply to certain nonmembers.

Registration Requirements by Risk Level

Level I: Lower Risk

  • Initial registration: Before release, or within three business days if not incarcerated
  • Registration updates: Every 90 days
  • Change of address: Report immediately in person to GRPD
  • Public notification: Information appears on the GRIC registry and the national registry

Level II: Intermediate Risk

  • Initial registration: Before release, or within three business days if not in custody
  • Registration updates: Every 90 days
  • Change of address: Report immediately in person to GRPD
  • Public notification: Information appears on the GRIC registry and the national registry

Level III: High Risk

  • Initial registration: Before release, or within three business days if not in custody
  • Registration updates: Every three months
  • Changes: Report immediately in person to GRPD
  • Community notification: Door-to-door notification flyers in the area where a Level III offender resides
  • Public registry: Information appears on the GRIC registry and the national registry
