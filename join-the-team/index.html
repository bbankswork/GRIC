The Sonoran Desert

Beautiful.Purposeful.Home.

The Gila River Indian Community sits in the heart of the Sonoran Desert, one of the most biodiverse deserts on Earth. Saguaro-studded mountains, dramatic sunsets, and 300+ days of sunshine are your daily backdrop.

As a GRPD officer you're not just starting a career, you're joining a community, protecting the sovereignty of the Community, and building a life in one of America's most dynamic regions.

Getting Started

The Hiring Process

No law enforcement experience needed. We walk you through every step of the way.

  1. 01

    Application

    Submit online with transcripts, diploma, and driving record.

  2. 02

    Physical Agility

    24 push-ups, 26 sit-ups per minute, and a 1.5-mile run under 15:20.

  3. 03

    Written Exam

    100-question exam covering law enforcement fundamentals.

  4. 04

    Oral Board

    Panel interview assessing communication skills and character.

  5. 05

    Background Check

    Thorough background investigation. Typically 3–6 months.

  1. 06

    Polygraph & Psych

    Polygraph exam and psychological evaluation.

  2. 07

    Medical & Drug Screen

    Medical examination and drug screening.

  3. 08

    Conditional Offer

    Conditional offer of employment.

  4. 09

    Command Interview

    Final interview with GRPD command staff.

  5. 10

    Academy & Training

    16-week paid academy followed by 18-week field training.
