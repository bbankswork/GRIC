K-9 Unit

Specialized Partners inPublic Safety

The Gila River Police Department's K-9 Unit brings specially trained police dogs and their handlers together to support law enforcement operations throughout the Gila River Indian Community.

Working as teams, K-9 officers and their handlers provide capabilities that can help officers locate evidence, search for individuals, and respond to situations requiring specialized assistance. Their training, teamwork, and discipline are important parts of the department's public safety efforts.

K-9 Unit

How the K-9 Unit Supports GRPD

👃

Narcotics Detection

Trained police dogs can assist officers in locating suspected illegal narcotics during authorized law enforcement operations.

🐾

Tracking and Searches

K-9 teams can support efforts to locate individuals and search designated areas.

🔍

Evidence Searches

A canine's sense of smell can help officers find concealed items or evidence that might otherwise be difficult to locate.

🚓

Patrol Support

K-9 teams may provide specialized assistance during patrol responses and other law enforcement incidents.

Training & Trust

A Partnership Built onTraining and Trust

An effective K-9 team depends on the working relationship between an officer and their canine partner. GRPD's K-9 program has historically included patrol training, narcotics detection training, obedience, and regular exercises to maintain coordinated responses.

Together, handlers and their dogs practice the skills needed to support fellow officers while maintaining control and safety during operations.

When Should You Contact GRPD?

When you contact GRPD, share the location and relevant details of the incident and follow the dispatcher's instructions.
