School Resource Officer Program

School Resource Officers

Supporting Safe Schools andStronger Connections

The Gila River Police Department's School Resource Officer (SRO) Program brings law enforcement officers into the school community to help support student safety and build positive relationships with young people, educators, and families.

School Resource Officers have a unique opportunity to be familiar, approachable faces for students while working with school staff on safety concerns and prevention education. Their work reflects GRPD's commitment to serving and protecting the Gila River Indian Community.

School Resource Officers

How School Resource Officers Support Our Community

🏫

School Safety

Work alongside school staff to help identify and address safety concerns and support a secure learning environment.

🤝

Student Relationships

Build trust with students through regular interaction, mentorship, and positive engagement.

📚

Prevention Education

Share age-appropriate information about making safe decisions, recognizing risks, and preventing harmful behavior.

🧑‍🏫

School Partnerships

Collaborate with educators and administrators on matters involving student and campus safety.

📋

Emergency Preparedness

Support school safety planning and coordination when appropriate, including participation in preparedness activities.

⭐

Youth Outreach

Help connect students with opportunities to learn about public safety and law enforcement careers.

Beyond the Classroom

Building Trust Beyondthe Classroom

GRPD School Resource Officers have a history of helping students learn about important safety topics and make positive choices. Gila River Indian News has documented officers' involvement in D.A.R.E. and G.R.E.A.T. prevention education, student mentorship, and summer first responder programs.

Those efforts demonstrate how relationships between officers and students can extend beyond immediate safety needs. Through conversations, learning opportunities, and shared experiences, officers can help young people become more familiar with the people who serve their Community.

Working Together for Student Safety

School safety depends on communication and cooperation among students, families, school personnel, and public safety partners. Students and families who have school-specific safety concerns should follow their school's established reporting procedures.
