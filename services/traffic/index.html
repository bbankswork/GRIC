Traffic Services

Keeping Our Roads andCommunity Safe

The Gila River Police Department works to promote safer travel throughout the Gila River Indian Community. Through traffic enforcement, roadway monitoring, and responses to traffic-related incidents, officers help protect drivers, passengers, pedestrians, and others using Community roads.

GRPD's Traffic Unit is one of the department's specialized law enforcement assignments. Traffic safety is also part of the work carried out by patrol officers throughout the Community.

Traffic Services

How GRPD Supports Traffic Safety

🚦

Traffic Law Enforcement

Officers enforce applicable traffic laws to address unsafe driving and promote safer roadways.

📡

Roadway Monitoring

Traffic monitoring helps officers identify and respond to roadway safety concerns.

🚑

Collision Response

Officers respond to reported traffic collisions, assist at incident scenes, and document events as appropriate.

🚫

Impaired Driving Enforcement

GRPD has participated in impaired driving enforcement efforts aimed at reducing alcohol- and drug-related driving risks.

🚧

Traffic Control and Roadway Assistance

Police may help manage traffic around incidents or other situations when needed.

🤝

Coordination with Law Enforcement Partners

GRPD works with neighboring and regional law enforcement agencies on public safety and traffic-related concerns.

Seven Districts

Serving Roads Acrossthe Community

The Gila River Indian Community spans seven districts across Pinal and Maricopa Counties. GRPD officers help address roadway safety concerns affecting residents, commuters, and visitors traveling through the Community.

By combining enforcement with public safety partnerships and community awareness, GRPD works to reduce dangerous driving behaviors and support safe travel.

What Should You Do About a Traffic Safety Concern?

If you are reporting a traffic incident, be prepared to share the location, direction of travel, nearby landmarks, and other relevant details if it is safe to do so.
