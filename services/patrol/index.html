Patrol Services

Protecting Our Community,Every Day

The Gila River Police Department provides law enforcement services 24 hours a day, seven days a week across the seven districts of the Gila River Indian Community. Patrol officers respond to calls for service, work to prevent crime, enforce applicable laws, and help protect people and property throughout the Community.

Through preventative and directed patrol, GRPD works to address public safety concerns while building positive relationships with the people it serves.

Patrol Services

What Do Patrol Officers Do?

📞

Respond to Calls for Service

Assist Community members and respond to reports of crimes, safety concerns, and other incidents that require police assistance.

🚓

Provide Preventative Patrol

Maintain a visible presence throughout the Community and remain alert to situations that may require police attention.

⚖️

Enforce Traffic and Criminal Laws

Enforce applicable traffic and criminal laws and respond to violations within the department's jurisdiction.

🛡️

Protect People and Property

Help safeguard residents, visitors, and public and private property across the Community.

🔍

Support Criminal Investigations

Document incidents, gather information, and coordinate with investigators when additional follow-up is needed.

🤝

Work with Law Enforcement Partners

Cooperate with tribal, state, federal, and neighboring law enforcement agencies when appropriate.

Seven Districts

Serving AllSeven Districts

GRPD patrol services extend across more than 583 square miles of the Gila River Indian Community, including roads, neighborhoods, and other areas where officers are needed. Because every district has different concerns, officers combine responsive policing with preventative efforts and problem-solving strategies.

Community engagement is also an important part of the department's approach. GRPD works with Community members to identify concerns, build trust, and improve safety throughout the seven districts.
