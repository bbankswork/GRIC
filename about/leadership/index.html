WilliamLewis

Acting Chief of Police

William Lewis has served the Gila River Indian Community for approximately 14 years since graduating from the ALEA Police Academy. Throughout his career, he has held roles including Patrol Officer, Field Training Officer, General Instructor, Ranger, Sergeant, Field Training Coordinator, Lieutenant, and Acting Commander. He currently serves as Acting Chief of Police, bringing experience across department operations and leadership to his role.

Lewis holds an Associate’s degree in Law Enforcement Technology from Rio Salado College, a Bachelor’s degree in Justice Studies with an emphasis in Criminal Justice Administration from Northern Arizona University, and a Master’s degree in Organizational Leadership with an emphasis in Criminal Justice Administration from Northern Arizona University, earned in December 2024. He is pursuing a doctoral degree at New York University and has completed the University of Chicago Police Leadership Academy and the FBI-LEEDA Trilogy.

A member of the Gila River Indian Community from Blackwater in District 1, Lewis currently resides in District 4. His personal ties to the Community inform his commitment to serving with cultural understanding, compassion, and a strong sense of responsibility.

Police Department

District Liaisons

The Gila River Police Department is committed to serving and protecting the Gila River Indian Community, which spans seven districts. Each district has a dedicated GRPD Officer who serves as a liaison between the department and community members. These officers work closely with district community groups focused on crime prevention, help organize neighborhood watch programs, and regularly attend district meetings to address local concerns.

At GRPD, we prioritize community engagement, aiming to build strong relationships while ensuring the well-being of all who live and work within the Gila River Indian Community. Together, we strive to create a safer, united community.
