Gila River Indian Community

Working Together for a Safer Community

The Gila River Police Department is committed to building strong relationships with the people we serve across all seven districts of the Gila River Indian Community. Through district liaisons, community education, neighborhood safety initiatives, and opportunities to connect with officers, we work together to promote safety, trust, and communication.

District Liaisons

Connecting Our Seven Districts

Each district has a dedicated GRPD sergeant who serves as a liaison between the Police Department and Community members. District liaisons attend community meetings, support crime prevention efforts, and help residents address neighborhood safety concerns.
