Citizens Police Academy

Gila River Citizens Police Academy

Building a Bridgeto Our Community

The goal of the Gila River Citizens Police Academy (CPA) is to build a bridge between members of the police department and the Gila River Indian Community it serves. The CPA also helps build positive relationships and improve communication between the department and Community members.

During the nine-week program, participants experience a civilian-adapted version of the police academy and gain insight into the life and training of a law enforcement officer.

Program Schedule

Nine Weeks, One Evening a Week

The Citizens Police Academy meets one evening per week for nine weeks, plus one longer Saturday session. Attendance matters, so plan for the full program before you apply.

Program Schedule

  • Nine-week program
  • One evening per week, typically Wednesday from 6:00 PM to 8:00 PM
  • One Saturday session scheduled for eight hours
  • Some classes may run longer
  • Participants may miss no more than two classes

Eligibility

Who Can Apply?

The Citizens Police Academy is open to Community members and spouses of Community members who live within the Community. All applicants complete a background check.

To be eligible, applicants must:

  • Be a member of the Gila River Indian Community, OR be the spouse of a Gila River Indian Community member and reside within the Community
  • Be 18 years of age or older
  • Have no felony or misdemeanor convictions
  • Complete a background check
