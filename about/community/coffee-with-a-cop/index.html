Coffee with a Cop

Coffee, Conversation,and Community

Building strong relationships starts with a conversation. Coffee with a Cop gives members of the Gila River Indian Community an opportunity to connect with GRPD officers in a relaxed, welcoming environment.

Whether you have a question, want to share a concern, or simply want to get to know the officers serving your Community, we're here to listen.
