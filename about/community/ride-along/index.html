What Is the Citizen Observer / Ride Along Program?

This program is designed to promote transparency and build trust within our Community. Participants have the opportunity to shadow our Police Officers and 911 Dispatchers, gaining a firsthand understanding of the roles they play in maintaining the security and well-being of the Gila River Indian Community.

Police Officer Observations

  • Ride along with an officer on patrol.
  • Observe officers responding to calls for service.
  • Observe officers interacting with Community members and making citizen contacts.
  • Observe the daily duties and challenges faced by our officers.

Dispatch Observations

  • Tour our Communications Center.
  • Sit with a Dispatcher and observe how calls for service are processed.
  • Gain insight into the role Dispatchers play in coordinating emergency responses.

How to Participate

Request a Ride Along

To request a ride along or Dispatch observation:

  1. 01

    Contact GRPD

    Contact the Gila River Police Department at (520) 562-7114 at least five working days before your preferred date.

  2. 02

    Complete the Forms

    Complete the required request forms and waivers. These will go through the department's approval process.

  3. 03

    Verify Your Identity

    Provide valid identification and consent to a Wants/Warrants check.

  4. 04

    Or Download the Form

    You may also download the official form, complete it, and return it to the department at least five working days before your requested date.
