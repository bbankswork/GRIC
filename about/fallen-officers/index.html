Honoring Those Who Served

The Gila River Police Department honors the officers who lost their lives in the line of duty while serving and protecting the Gila River Indian Community.

Their courage, dedication, and sacrifice will always be remembered. We recognize their service, the families and loved ones they left behind, and the lasting impact they made on our department and Community.
