About Us

Enforcing Tribal, State, and Federal Laws with Integrity and Respect

The Gila River Police Department (GRPD) is a full-service, 24/7 law enforcement agency committed to the protection of life, property, and the sovereignty of the Gila River Indian Community. We are entrusted with enforcing tribal, state, and federal laws across the Community’s seven districts, which span more than 583 square miles in both Pinal and Maricopa Counties, adjacent to the Phoenix metropolitan area.

GRPD officers are empowered to investigate and present cases in tribal, state, and federal courts as appropriate. We work closely with other tribal, state, and federal law enforcement agencies to ensure comprehensive public safety services.

Certifications & Commissions

  • Arizona Peace Officer Standards and Training Board (AZPOST)
  • Bureau of Indian Affairs - Office of Justice Services

About GRPD

Who We Are

We are a team of over 186 sworn officers and 71 civilian professionals committed to excellence in policing. From the Chief of Police down, we emphasize professionalism, accountability, and responsiveness to the community’s needs. We take great care in selecting personnel who embody our department’s core values and are dedicated to serving with integrity and respect.

Our Mission

“It is the Mission of the Gila River Police Department to protect the life, property and sovereignty of all members of the Community by responding to calls for service with Integrity, proactively looking to prevent crime and to improve the quality of life of those we serve.”

What We Do

Our approach is rooted in transparency, collaborative partnerships, and effective communication. Through our commitment to community policing, we aim to enhance the quality of life and safety across the Gila River Indian Community.

Law Enforcement Services

  • Proactive patrol and crime prevention
  • Criminal investigations
  • Community engagement and outreach
  • Directed enforcement and problem-solving strategies
